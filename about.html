About Us

Mido's computer shop is a small retail store that specialises in computer and hardware sales. Our focus is to create a website for our store to market its products and provide important business information to our online customers. Mido’s computer shop was established in 2019 in Mthatha, growing from a small, semi-developed start up to a leading SA online retailer of computer hardware and tech accessories, driven by a zest for tech and great services.

Our mission is to provide people with the best tech, prices and guidance. We envision being the go-to tech hub in Africa and all around the world, providing the best connection and services through our products. If you are part of the tech-savvy crowd in South Africa and abroad then this is the right place for you.
